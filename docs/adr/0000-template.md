## Why a record

What makes this choice significant: hard to reverse, costly, or shaping other parts of the product.

## Context

The need and the constraints: subject section, selected features, measured facts.

## Alternatives

- **Option A (chosen):** what it is, what it gives us here.
- **Option B:** why not for this project, and what the product would look like with it.
- **Option C:** same.

## Decision

What we do, in 1 to 3 sentences, with the exact values (versions, limits, settings).

## Consequences

- What it costs us, its known limits, and why we accept them.
- **Fact:** a claim taken from documentation. ([Source title](https://example.com))

## Evidence

Proof of concept, measurements, links to exploratory code, with the numbers and how we got them.
